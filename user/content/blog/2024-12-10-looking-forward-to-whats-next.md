Every new beginning comes with a mixture of excitement and uncertainty.

Starting a new project, learning a skill, changing direction, or pursuing an idea means stepping into something you haven't completely figured out yet.

And that's okay.

## You Don't Need the Entire Plan

It's easy to believe you need to know exactly where you're going before you begin.

Usually, you don't. You simply need to know the next step.

Take that step, learn from what happens, and decide what comes next.

## Keep Moving Forward

Progress isn't always obvious.

Sometimes it happens quickly. Other times it happens slowly through hundreds of small decisions.

Celebrate the milestones, learn from the mistakes, and keep going.

The next chapter hasn't been written yet. That's what makes it exciting.
