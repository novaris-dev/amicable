Everyone gets the same twenty-four hours each day, yet it often feels like there isn't enough time.

The problem isn't always a lack of time. Sometimes it's that our attention gets divided among too many things.

## Identify Your Priorities

Ask yourself what deserves your time right now.

Your list might include family, health, work, friends, learning, rest, or personal projects.

Your priorities don't need to look like anyone else's.

## Protect Your Time

Once you know what's important, make space for it.

That might mean saying no to something else, turning off notifications, or simply scheduling time for something you've been meaning to do.

Time is limited, which is exactly why deciding how to use it matters.
