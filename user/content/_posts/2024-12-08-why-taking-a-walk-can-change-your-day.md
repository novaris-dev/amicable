Walking is one of the simplest activities available to us.

You don't need special equipment, a complicated routine, or much preparation. You simply step outside and start moving.

## A Change of Environment

When you've been sitting at a desk or looking at a screen for hours, even a short walk can provide a welcome change.

You notice things you might otherwise miss: the weather, neighborhood sounds, trees, buildings, and people going about their day.

Walking also creates space to think without constantly staring at another screen.

## Start With Ten Minutes

You don't need to walk for miles.

Start with ten minutes. Walk around the block, through a nearby park, or simply down the street and back.

Sometimes the smallest break can make the biggest difference.
